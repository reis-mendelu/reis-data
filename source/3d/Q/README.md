# 3D model: budova Q (PEF)

The app's building card shows Q in 3D, cut open at a lesson's floor. The model is
generated by `scripts/buildQModel.mjs` into `map/3d/Q.glb` + `map/3d/Q.json`
(served through jsDelivr like the room outlines). Regenerate after any change here:

```
node scripts/buildQModel.mjs
node --test scripts/*.test.mjs
```

The test fails if the committed model is stale, over budget (1.5 MB, 20k
triangles, no textures or compression), or if any Q room outline in
`map/rooms-0.geojson` lies more than 1.5 m outside the shell.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `brno-lod2-parts.json` | Q's six building parts from Brno's LOD2 model (RÚIAN stavební objekt 45515174): footprint ring, `baseheight` 231.66 m, `bldgheight` 7.4–28.8 m, all flat roofs. With flat roofs, footprint + base + height is the complete LOD2 geometry. Query URL inside the file. |
| `terrain-samples.json` | ČÚZK DMR 5G heights on a 6×6 grid around Q (223–242 m). The generator fits a plane to the samples within 90 m, which the app draws as the ground. The ground falls ~10 m to the west, so floors −2 and −1 open onto the west side and are buried on the east. |

## What is derived here, not measured

- **Storey elevations** (`Q_STOREYS`): fitted to the parts' step heights — west
  wing roof 22.18 m tops the 3rd floor, east 25.62 m the 4th, south 28.75 m the
  5th, 3.35 m per storey; podium roofs 12.10/12.79 m top floor 0 — and to the
  terrain (east ground ≈ floor 0). A DWG section from MENDELU's Stavební oddělení
  would replace these with measured values.
- **Facade finishing** (`Q_STYLES`): colours and window bands chosen from
  Wikimedia Commons photos — "Letecký pohled na budovu Q (PEF MENDELU).jpg" and
  "South view of Faculty of Business and Economics MENDELU in Brno". Used as
  reference only; no photo is in this repository or the model.
- **Curved north front** (`Q_CURVED_FRONT`): the north block's silver roof sweeps
  down towards the north past its flat top. LOD2 keeps only the flat top, which
  left lecture halls Q01–Q03 5.2 m outside the model; the generator adds a
  5.5 m barrel-vaulted front back.
